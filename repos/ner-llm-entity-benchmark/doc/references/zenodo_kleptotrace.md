# Zenodo - Kleptotrace (Record 14027005)

**Full Name:** Zenodo Record 14027005 - Kleptotrace Dataset
**Category:** Primary
**Relationship to the Project:** This record serves as the official archival source for the Kleptotrace dataset, providing the empirical raw data used to establish the benchmark's ground truth for financial crime and corruption-related NER.
**Link:** [https://zenodo.org/record/14027005](https://zenodo.org/record/14027005)
**Description:** An open-access research repository containing the Kleptotrace dataset, which focuses on the identification and tracking of financial illicit activities and corruption within complex textual data.
