# HuggingFace - Trade Screening

**Full Name:** emperor-mew/trade-screening (HuggingFace Dataset)
**Category:** Primary
**Relationship to the Project:** Provides targeted data for trade-based money laundering (TBML) and trade screening, expanding the benchmark's scope beyond general sanctions to specific commercial trade compliance.
**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/datasets/emperor-mew/trade-screening](https://huggingface.co/datasets/emperor-mew/trade-screening)
**Description:** A HuggingFace dataset containing annotated examples of trade screening and monitoring, designed to test the precision of entity extraction in trade-related compliance documents.
