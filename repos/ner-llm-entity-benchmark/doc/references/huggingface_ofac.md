# HuggingFace - OFAC Recent

**Full Name:** emperor-mew/ofac-recent (HuggingFace Dataset)
**Category:** Primary
**Relationship to the Project:** Used to provide high-fidelity, recent sanctions data from the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), allowing the benchmark to evaluate the model's capability to recognize current high-risk entities.
**Link:** [https://huggingface.co/datasets/emperor-mew/ofac-recent](https://huggingface.co/datasets/emperor-mew/ofac-recent)
**Description:** A specialized dataset hosted on HuggingFace containing recent entries from OFAC sanctions lists, formatted for NER and compliance screening tasks.
