# References and Bibliography

This document serves as the central index for all sources used in the NER-LLM Entity Benchmark project, organized according to academic standards for thesis documentation.

## 1. Categorization by Origin and Content

### Primary Sources
*Original, first-hand evidence or raw data.*
- **Zenodo (Kleptotrace)**: Official archival source of benchmark raw data. [Details](./zenodo_kleptotrace.md)
- **GitHub (Kleptotrace Implementation)**: Implementation and data distribution for the dataset. [Details](./github_kleptotrace.md)
- **HuggingFace (OFAC Dataset)**: Recent sanctions data for high-fidelity NER testing. [Details](./huggingface_ofac.md)
- **HuggingFace (Trade Screening Dataset)**: Specialized trade screening data for compliance scope expansion. [Details](./huggingface_trade.md)

### Secondary Sources
*Interpretations, analyses, or evaluations of primary sources.*
- **Academic Literature Review**: Synthesis of peer-reviewed papers on LLM-NER and AML compliance. [Details](./academic_literature_review.md)
- **OpenSanctions Justification**: Analysis of the data provenance and academic validity of the OpenSanctions ecosystem. [Details](./opensanctions_justification.md)

### Tertiary Sources
*Compilations, indexes, or digests.*
- **OpenSanctions Index**: Reference index and ground-truth validation for legal entities. [Details](./opensanctions.md)
- **Ollama Infrastructure**: Local LLM serving engine providing the computational substrate. [Details](./ollama.md)
